# Business, Banking & International Trade Projects

I’m a student interested in banking, international business and how companies manage money, trade and operations.

I started building these projects to learn by actually working with Excel and Power BI rather than only studying the theory.

## My Projects

### 1. ASOS Business Banking Analysis — Excel

I analysed ASOS from a business banking perspective.

The project covers:
- Financial performance
- FX exposure
- Cash management
- Working capital
- International payments
- Trade finance
- SWIFT payments
- Banking recommendations

**What I learned:** how a company's international operations can create different banking needs, from managing currencies and cash to reducing payment and trade risks.

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

---

### 2. Import & Export Operations Dashboard — Excel

I built a shipment and logistics dashboard to track international import and export activity.

It includes:
- Shipment status
- Freight costs
- Shipping lines
- Customs clearance
- Payment status
- ETD / ETA
- Logistics KPIs

**What I learned:** how shipment, payment and logistics information can be brought together to understand international trade operations.

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

---

### 3. UK–India–UAE Trade Corridor Analysis — Power BI

I analysed trade between the UK, India and UAE from 2020–2024.

The project looks at:
- Trade values
- Trade growth
- UK–India vs UK–UAE corridors
- Banking implications
- Trade finance products
- Business recommendations

I also connected the trade data to banking areas such as payments, FX, trade finance and working capital.

**What I learned:** how trade data can be turned into a dashboard and then linked to real business and banking decisions.

**Tool:** Power BI
